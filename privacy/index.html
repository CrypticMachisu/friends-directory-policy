Privacy policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026 · Applies to: Friends & Family Directory, all versions up to and including 1.0.3 (Android app and web preview build)

The short version

The Friends & Family Directory is a local-first personal diary and contact directory. Everything you record — people, moments, photos, voice transcripts, key dates — lives only on your own device. The app has no server, no accounts, no analytics, and no telemetry. The developers cannot read your diary, and there is nothing to breach, because nothing is sent anywhere by the app itself.

The one honest exception you should know about: voice dictation. When you dictate an entry, your speech is processed by your device's built-in speech recognition (Android system service, or your browser's speech engine on web). That processing is governed by your operating system or browser vendor — not by this app. See "Voice dictation" below.

What the app stores, and where

All app content is kept in a local database and local files on your device:

  • People records — names, nicknames, relationships, birthdays, notes, tags, key dates.
  • Moments — diary entries, categories, timestamps, links to people.
  • Photos — profile photos and moment photos (up to three per moment), stored as files in the app's private storage on-device.
  • Spotted facts, vitals, and patterns — the structured notes the app suggests back to you.
  • Settings — reminders, preferences.

On Android this data sits in the app's private internal storage, which other apps and other users of the device cannot read. Nothing is uploaded, synced, or backed up to any cloud service by the app.

What the app does NOT do

  • No account. No sign-up, no login, no identity of any kind.
  • No analytics or telemetry. The app makes no usage reports, crash reports, or tracking calls.
  • No advertising. No ad SDKs, no identifiers collected for ads.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There is no data to sell: the app collects nothing and transmits nothing.
  • No cookies or web trackers. The app is not a website; it makes no third-party embeds.

Permissions the app uses

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — only to power the mic button in the capture sheet. Access is requested by the system speech-recognition plugin when you first tap the mic and denied means dictation is simply unavailable; every other feature works normally.
  • Camera and storage access — only when you add a photo, to take a picture or read the gallery image you pick.
  • Internet — declared as a baseline capability for the Capacitor runtime and web builds. The app itself performs no network requests.

Voice dictation

When you tap the mic and speak:

  • On Android, the community speech-recognition plugin hands your audio to the Android system speech service (typically Google's, per your device settings). What that service retains and how it processes your voice is governed by Google's privacy policy and your device settings, not by this app.
  • On web builds, the browser's built-in SpeechRecognition engine is used, and its handling of audio is governed by your browser vendor's terms (for example, Chrome may route audio to Google's servers for transcription).

What this app itself does with the result: the final transcript is inserted into the entry text in the capture sheet and stored in the local database only, exactly like typed text. The app never stores audio — only the recognized text — and never transmits the transcript anywhere.

If you never tap the mic, no speech processing of any kind occurs.

Exports and sharing

Exporting your diary (Settings → Your data) creates a JSON file containing your people, moments, and photos and hands it to the Android share sheet or your browser's download flow. You choose where it goes; the app does not upload it. Treat exported files as sensitive — they contain everything in your diary in plain form.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting a person, moment, or photo removes it from the on-device database immediately.
  • Settings → Your data can restore or replace the whole database.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all app data, photos, and settings from the device. There is no copy on any server to clean up afterwards, because there is no server.

Children

The app is a general-audience personal tool with no age gate, no accounts, and no data collection. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — it cannot: nothing leaves the device.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds any network capability (cloud backup, sync, crash reporting), this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the release notes will call the change out. The policy's effective date will change with every material revision; prior versions are preserved in the app's public source repository history.
